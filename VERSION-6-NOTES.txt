AI Caseworker Version 6

Speech architecture
- Main STT recognizer is fixed to the current session language for better short-answer accuracy.
- A second shadow recognizer uses Azure auto language detection only for language-switch requests.
- Shadow STT never becomes the normal intake answer transcript.
- Supported switch detector languages: en-US, fa-IR, fr-CA.
- Phrases such as "I'm married" stay with the English main recognizer even if auto-LID briefly guesses another language.
- Persian requests such as "من انگلیسی بلد نیستم" or "فارسی صحبت کن" can trigger a switch to fa-IR.
- French requests such as "je ne parle pas anglais" or "en français" can trigger a switch to fr-CA.
- After a confirmed switch, the main STT recognizer restarts in the new fixed language and the current question is repeated in that language.
- Persistent barge-in remains enabled.
