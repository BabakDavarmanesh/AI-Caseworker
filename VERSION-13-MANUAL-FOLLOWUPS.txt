Version 13 — Manual Client Intake Follow-ups

Changes:
- Follow-up questions are shown in the main question card at the top of Client Intake.
- Follow-up playback no longer starts automatically.
- Microphone recording starts only when Speak is clicked.
- Recording ends only when Stop is clicked; silence no longer ends the recording automatically.
- Pressing Stop still finalizes the transcript and triggers answer analysis.
- Play Question plays the currently active prompt (main question or current follow-up).
- Duplicate follow-up text in the lower AI Analysis/workflow area is hidden.
