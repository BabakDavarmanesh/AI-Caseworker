Version 14 - Final follow-up wait fix

- The third follow-up no longer causes the intake to advance merely because it was displayed.
- Each follow-up now requires a fresh client speech response before analysis is accepted.
- Separate counters track follow-ups displayed and follow-up answers actually received.
- Duplicate/stale Analyze calls cannot consume Follow-up 3/3.
- After the client answers Follow-up 3/3, the answer is analyzed; only then can the item be flagged for review and advanced if still incomplete.
