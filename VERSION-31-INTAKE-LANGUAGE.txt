VERSION 31 - Client Intake: interview language

- New "Interview language" bar at the top of Client Intake: English plus every language
  that has translations in the Translation Engine (partial ones show e.g. 2/4).
  The choice is remembered.
- With a language selected:
    * the question is shown in that language (RTL for Persian/Arabic) with the English
      text underneath ("EN ...");
    * main questions use the Translation Engine text (including manual edits); AI
      follow-ups and workflow prompts are translated on the fly;
    * Play Question speaks in that language with a native Azure neural voice;
    * Speak recognizes speech in that language;
    * guardrail notices are spoken in that language.
- Answers stay in English where it matters: the analysis returns the English translation
  and an English mapped value (rule added to both analysis prompts); responses store
  englishTranslation / mappedAnswer in English, keep the client's original words in
  detectedClientSpeech, and record interviewLanguage.
- English works exactly as before.

Files: js/client-intake.js (language module, prompt/playback/analysis hooks),
js/app.js (speech recognition language for Client Intake listeners, speakTextInLanguage,
renderConv language bar, English-value rule in analysis prompts), css/app.css (appended).
